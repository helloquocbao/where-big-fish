WHAT YOU WILL FIND

One world. Different waters.

Where Big Fish is a real-time fishing game built around exploration, a hands-on reeling minigame and collecting different catches. You aim and charge each cast yourself. When a fish bites, holding raises your catch zone and releasing lets it fall. Your job is to follow the fish as it moves.

The lakes have different catch pools. The East Sea includes ocean fish, while inland waters offer their own mix. Other anglers share the map; NPC fishers also populate the shores. Your catches add to a session score and a field guide, giving you a reason to try another spot.

A Leviathan encounter adds a cooperative challenge: nearby players can assist, but a failed fight puts the team's session at risk. Read the boss guide before joining one.

Good to know

Is the game free?

Yes. You can enter a name and play in a modern browser without downloading an app.

Will my progress be saved?

Your score and fish collection belong to your current session. There is no account-based progress saving. Your browser remembers your chosen name, skin and sound preference.

Can I play on my phone?

Touch controls provide a joystick and a cast/reel button. Gameplay uses landscape orientation; rotate your phone when prompted. Desktop players can use WASD or arrow keys and a mouse.

What happens if the connection drops?

You may return to the entry screen. Session progress is not guaranteed to survive a disconnect. Check your connection before joining a boss encounter.
